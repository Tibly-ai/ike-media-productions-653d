Selected work

Four ways we shoot a property.

01

Listing films

A 60 to 90 second film that carries a buyer through the house in order: approach, entry, living space, kitchen, primary suite, outdoor. Shot on a gimbal with motivated light, cut to music, and delivered in horizontal and vertical so the same shoot feeds the MLS and the feed.

02

Guided property tours

You on camera, walking the space and talking through what makes it worth the asking price. We handle audio, lighting, and a shot list built around your talking points, then intercut the b-roll so the tour holds attention past the first ten seconds.

03

Agent brand films

The film that runs on your site and in your listing presentations. Interview-led, documentary in feel, built around how you work and who you work for. Sellers hire the person before they hire the brokerage.

04

Builder and development stories

Coverage across a build, from framing to finished model home, edited into a story a developer can use for pre-sales, investor decks, and the next phase of the neighborhood.

How it runs

Concept to final cut, in one week or less.

Real estate moves on a clock. The process below is built so your film is ready while the listing is still new.

  1. 01

    Walk-through

    We look at the property or the floor plan, track the light through the day, and build the shot list around the rooms that sell it.

  2. 02

    Shoot day

    Two to five hours on site for most homes. Cinema camera, gimbal movement, aerials where the lot deserves them, and clean interview audio when you are on camera.

  3. 03

    Edit and color

    Story-first assembly, music licensed for commercial use, and a color pass that keeps whites clean and wood tones honest.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Horizontal master for the listing and your site, vertical cutdowns for Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts, plus stills pulled from the footage. One round of revisions included.
