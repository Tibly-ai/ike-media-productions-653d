About the founder

Hi, I'm Izac. Filmmaker.

It started with baseball.

My journey into visual storytelling started with my first love: baseball. That passion led me to earn a scholarship to play at the Academy of Art University in San Francisco, where I received my B.A. in Communications with a minor in Film in 2018.

In 2016, I picked up my first camera. In 2023, I launched Ike Media Productions, a full service creative studio dedicated to visual storytelling with purpose.

I've worked across music, real estate, non-profits, beauty, health and fitness, automotive, weddings, and sports, bringing a blend of strategy and heart to every project. I believe great storytelling is equal parts craft and connection. Brand campaign, documentary-style piece, or social content, I work to make sure your story is told in a way that feels honest, cinematic, and effective.

At the core of what I do is a simple mission: tell stories the best way possible, and help my clients grow their brand and revenue through powerful, intentional content.
