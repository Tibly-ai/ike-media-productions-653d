Music
Video

Performance, narrative, and visualizer pieces made with artists. Every cut is built around the track: where the light lands, how the camera moves, and when the edit breathes.

Tap any frame to view it full screen.

Three ways we shoot music

Range in lighting, movement, and edit rhythm.

01

Performance

The artist, the song, and light that moves with it. We build performance pieces around lens choice and camera motion, then cut to the pocket of the track so the energy on set is the energy on screen.

02

Narrative

A story cut to music. We start with the lyric, write a treatment with you, then handle casting notes, locations, and shot lists so the scenes carry weight instead of just filling time between choruses.

03

Visualizer & Social

Loops, teasers, and vertical cutdowns for release week. Shot in the same session as the main video so a single day of production feeds the rollout across every platform.
