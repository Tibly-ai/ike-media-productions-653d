The studio

Ike Media Productions is a full service video production company based in Austin, Texas. From pre-production to final cut, we approach every collaboration with care and intent. Let's create something meaningful together.

I'm Izac, a storyteller and filmmaker. I picked up my first camera in 2016, earned a B.A. in Communications with a minor in Film in 2018, and launched the studio in 2023. Since then I've worked across music, real estate, non-profits, beauty, health and fitness, automotive, weddings, and sports.
